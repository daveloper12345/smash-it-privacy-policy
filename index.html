Smash It! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 May 2026

Smash It! is a personal backlog and task management app. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data Collection

Smash It! does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data outside of your local device. All data you enter (tasks, notes, images) is stored locally on your device only and never sent to any server.

Permissions

  • Camera/Media access - only used if you choose to attach a photo to a task. Photos are stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications - used to remind you of upcoming tasks. Notification content is generated from your local task data.
  • Receive Boot Completed - used to reschedule your notifications after the device restarts.
  • App settings (such as notification preferences) are stored locally on your device using your device's standard storage.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
